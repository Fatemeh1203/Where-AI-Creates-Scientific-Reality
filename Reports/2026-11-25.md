#### 📝 Summary / خلاصه
Derivative-free optimization (DFO) methods are popular for solving stochastic optimization problems, with model-based methods being a practically successful class of DFO algorithm. In most existing works, sufficiently accurate objective estimates (used for model construction and to test for sufficient decrease) are constructed by taking a sample mean of several noisy evaluations. In this work, we demonstrate that using robust estimators (specifically the median, trimmed mean, and median-of-means) can improve the sample complexity of model-based DFO methods. Our numerical results demonstrate that robust estimators are also a practical choice for algorithm implementations.
