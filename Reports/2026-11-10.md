#### 📝 Summary / خلاصه
Contact-rich robotic insertion is challenging because uncertainty in alignment, friction, and contact conditions can lead to unsuccessful assembly, excessive contact forces, and jamming. Designing a reliable controller therefore requires balancing insertion performance, robustness, and recovery from unexpected contact. In this paper, we propose PSO+TR, a derivative-free optimization (DFO) method that combines the exploratory search of particle swarm optimization (PSO) with a trust-region (TR) procedure for local refinement to improve the search process when progress stagnates. We apply the method to derivative-free policy optimization of an interpretable robotic insertion controller, jointly tuning its motion, feedback, and recovery parameters while preserving its predefined control structure. The proposed approach is evaluated on 93 eligible benchmark problems from the bound-constrained programs (BCP) collection and a synthetic contact-rich planar insertion task under uncertain operating conditions. Three random seeds per benchmark problem yield 279 matched problem--seed instances per method. PSO+TR reaches the prescribed target on 228 of these instances (81.72\%), the highest observed target-attainment rate among the five compared methods, which also include PSO, randomized black-box optimization (VRBBO), matrix adaptation evolution strategy (MAES), and differential evolution (DE). Across eight robotic optimizer seeds, the deployed controller-selection pipeline achieves 97.78\% completion under development conditions and 58.00\% under withheld stress conditions, compared with 91.22\% and 31.12\%, respectively, for the fixed staged controller. The stress violation rate is reduced from 20.69\% to 2.19\%. These findings support combining global and local search for DFO and structured contact-rich controller tuning. The synthetic planar results require validation on physical robots.

### 2. Random-subspace Ellipsoidal Trust-region Methods for Derivative-free Optimization
**عنوان (فارسی):** Random-subspace Ellipsoidal Trust-region Methods for Derivative-free Optimization

- **Authors / نویسندگان:** Yiwen Chen, Pengcheng Xie

- **Rating / امتیاز:** ★★★★☆ (4/5)

#### 📝 Summary / خلاصه
Random-subspace model-based methods offer a promising approach to solving derivative-free optimization (DFO) problems in high dimensions. However, the usual ratio of actual to predicted reduction assesses model agreement at the trial point without directly measuring subspace quality. An accurate prediction in a poorly aligned subspace may therefore trigger trust-region expansion in all directions. We introduce Random-subspace Ellipsoidal TRust-region Optimization (RETRO), a framework for unconstrained DFO using ellipsoidal trust regions. Its trust-region updates combine global size changes with additional expansion or contraction within the sampled subspace, reflecting where the trial step was computed. We define an ellipsoidal subspace alignment measure that accounts for both the subspace and trust-region shape, and analyze Haar-uniform sampling and metric-guided sampling based on the current shape matrix. We establish almost-sure first-order liminf convergence and an $\mathcal{O}(ε^{-2})$ bound on the expected number of iterations required to reach an iterate with gradient norm at most $ε$. Numerical experiments on projected low-rank problems in dimension $1000$ show that using ellipsoidal shape information in subspace selection can reduce function evaluation costs relative to ball-based QARSTA, with the most pronounced improvements occurring when the solver subspace dimension matches the projection rank.

### 3. Semblance Distance Analysis for Channel Quality Assessment in Distributed Acoustic Sensing
**عنوان (فارسی):** Semblance Distance Analysis for Channel Quality Assessment in Distributed Acoustic Sensing

- **Open Access:** Yes (preprint) · **Citations:** — · **IF:** — · **Quartile:** —

- **Rating / امتیاز:** ★★★★☆ (4/5)

#### 📝 Summary / خلاصه
Distributed Acoustic Sensing (DAS) enables dense, kilometer-scale measurements for seismic and environmental monitoring, but its performance is limited by spatially heterogeneous noise and fiber to ground coupling. Identifying high-quality sensing channels remains a challenge, particularly in nonstationary and complex noise environments where conventional signal to noise ratio (SNR) or correlation based approaches are insufficient. In this work, we introduce a statistical framework for DAS channel quality assessment based on a semblance distance metric, which quantifies the deviation of individual channels from a locally coherent reference signal. Unlike traditional pairwise similarity measures, the proposed method formulates channel selection as a distance estimation problem in a coherence enhanced signal space, enabling identification of incoherent noise and coupling induced distortions without requiring prior knowledge of installation conditions or noise statistics. The method is validated using multiple independent datasets, including recordings of the 2023 Turkish-Syrian earthquakes, a controlled seismic source experiment, and long-term continuous measurements. Across all scenarios, the approach identifies regions of low noise and strong coupling, with results that correlate with independent SNR and root mean square error (RMSE) metrics. Furthermore, the method detects subtle sub-decibel temporal variations in coupling conditions over seasonal timescales, demonstrating its capability for continuous fiber health monitoring. These results establish semblance distance analysis as a physically interpretable tool for DAS channel selection with direct applications in seismic imaging, structural health monitoring, and noise mitigation in high sensitivity systems such as gravitational wave detectors.

### 4. Fading Suppression in Distributed Acoustic Sensing Using Frequency-Domain Replica Reconstruction and Noise-Whitened Principal Component Combining
**عنوان (فارسی):** Fading Suppression in Distributed Acoustic Sensing Using Frequency-Domain Replica Reconstruction and Noise-Whitened Principal Component Combining

- **Authors / نویسندگان:** Ziang Chen, Zhiyang Xue, Huan Huang, Yuhan Jiang, Dongdong Zou, Gangxiang Shen, Yi Cai
- **Affiliation / وابستگی:** — · **Country / کشور:** —
- **Venue / منبع:** arXiv (preprint) — arXiv · **Preprint**

- **Link:** http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34224v1 · **Scholar:** [search](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Fading+Suppression+in+Distributed+Acoustic+Sensing+Using+Frequency-Domain+Replica+Reconstruction+and+Noise-Whitened+Principal+Component+Combining)
- **Open Access:** Yes (preprint) · **Citations:** — · **IF:** — · **Quartile:** —
- **Research Area / حوزه:** Distributed Acoustic Sensing (DAS)
- **Keywords / کلیدواژه‌ها:** eess.SP
- **Rating / امتیاز:** ★★★★☆ (4/5)

#### 📝 Summary / خلاصه
Frequency-selective Rayleigh fading in distributed acoustic sensing (DAS) based on phase-sensitive optical time-domain reflectometry (phi-OTDR) calls for effective diversity, but existing rotated-vector-sum (RVS) combining of nonoverlapping subbands incurs a spatial-resolution penalty. In this letter, we propose frequency-domain replica reconstruction and noise-whitened principal component combining (PCC) that combines a full-band replica and overlapping subbands using fixed weights estimated across probing periods after whitening correlated replica noise. This approach retains wider subbands and reduces reliance on noisy first-period alignment, balancing diversity with nominal spatial resolution. Experiments over approximately 7.1 km of fiber with 16-microsecond linear frequency-modulated (LFM) pulses recover 500-Hz vibrations with reduced background phase excursions and a 2.46-2.76-dB improvement in the vibration-to-background ratio (VBR) over the first-period RVS.

#### 📝 Summary / خلاصه
Generalized disjunctive programming (GDP) describes process design alternatives with Boolean variables, disjunctions, and logical propositions, and fixing one configuration leaves a subproblem in the continuous variables. Conventional GDP methods require explicit equations, which a simulator may not expose, and mixed-integer nonlinear programming (MINLP) reformulations can be intractable for nonconvex models. The external-variable reformulation turns configuration choice into an optimization over an integer lattice whose objective is the subproblem value, which derivative-free optimization (DFO) can search while the GDP model continues describing the alternatives. We pair GDP models with surrogate DFO methods and the direct-search method NOMAD and compare them with discrete steepest descent (D-SDA), logic-based and MINLP solvers, and enumeration on nine process models, 21 test functions, a pharmaceutical flowsheet simulated in PharmaPy, and a catalytic distillation column with 100,947 configurations. In the flowsheet example, where GDP solvers do not apply, RBFOpt and MISO find the best of 36 simulated configurations in every run with 20 evaluations. For the catalytic column, D-SDA and MISO reach the best known design with 100 evaluations, whereas MINLP solvers find no design within 15% of it and global solvers return contradictory certificates. This search pays off when the configuration space is too large to enumerate, has deceptive local minima, or has MINLP reformulations that fail, and enumeration or a direct reformulation is simpler for small algebraic models.
